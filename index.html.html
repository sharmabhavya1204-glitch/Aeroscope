Why land monitoring arrives too late

Decision-makers need a quick, light alert, not a pile of raw raster files.

6+ months

Reporting lag

Surveys and census reports take months. By the time clearing is reported, illegal logging or encroachment cannot be undone.

> 55%

Cloud blindness

Tropical and monsoon regions sit under cloud for over half the year, hiding optical satellites during peak deforestation.

100+ GB

Download waste

Full-scene GeoTIFFs of 1 GB or more per tile fill disks and slow every analysis down.

How AeroScope works

A four-stage cloud pipeline turns raw orbital pixels into alerts a ranger or town planner can act on the same day.

  1. Stream

    Query STAC catalogs and read only the needed byte ranges of Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs, cutting network use by up to 95%.

    pystac-client · Rasterio

  2. Clean

    Mask clouds and shadows, filter radar speckle, and align scenes captured on different dates.

    NumPy · Xarray

  3. Detect

    Compare today with a multi-year baseline so harvest cycles are not mistaken for forest loss.

    PyTorch Geo · U-Net

  4. Alert

    Send vector polygons with confidence scores to dashboards, webhooks and mobile alerts.

    FastAPI · PostGIS

Three satellites, one picture

Sentinel-2 optical

10–20 m resolution, 13 bands, 5-day revisit. Best for vegetation and built-up mapping.

Sentinel-1 radar

C-band SAR sees through cloud, smog and darkness. Detects canopy removal and new structures.

Landsat 8/9 archive

Forty years of history at 30 m sets long-term norms and separates drought from lasting damage.

Deforestation

NDVI = (B8 − B4) / (B8 + B4)

A drop below 0.3 plus lower radar backscatter triggers a felling alert.

Urban sprawl

NDBI = (B11 − B8) / (B11 + B8)

Concrete and roofing reflect more in SWIR, so rising NDBI marks new construction.

Coastal erosion

MNDWI = (B3 − B11) / (B3 + B11)

Separates water from sand to measure shoreline retreat in metres per year.

An alert fires only when the change exceeds three standard deviations from the historical median, which keeps false alarms low.
